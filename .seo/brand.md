# CiteGuild — Brand Context for SEO

> Read every time. This file is read by every phase of the SEO sprint.

## Product

- **Name:** CiteGuild
- **One-liner (≤20 words):** CiteGuild makes member articles discoverable when AI writing agents need a relevant source.
- **What we do:** Members add public sitemaps. CiteGuild indexes eligible articles and lets writing agents retrieve relevant sources through MCP, CLI, or API. Agents decide whether a source helps; CiteGuild records member-to-member links later observed on published pages.
- **Pricing structure:** One $10 monthly plan for unlimited legitimate sitemap-backed sites.
- **Free tier?** No — no free plan, trial, annual plan, agency tier, or per-site pricing.

## Audience

- **Primary persona:** Independent SaaS and content publishers who produce useful, source-worthy articles.
- **Secondary personas:** AI writing-agent builders, content teams, developer-marketers, and ethical SEO practitioners.
- **Industries we target:** SaaS, developer tools, independent publishing, technical education, and content-led businesses.
- **Company size we target:** Solo publishers through small content teams.
- **Jobs to be done (top 3):**
  1. Make a site's useful articles available to writing agents without repeated outreach.
  2. Give agents a relevance-ranked source index they can query through MCP, CLI, or API.
  3. Observe member-to-member citations without claiming or forcing attribution.

## Brand voice

- **Voice tags:** honest, technical, plainspoken, relevance-first, no-hype.
- **Person/perspective:** “we” for CiteGuild; lead with what the reader or agent can do.
- **Forbidden words/phrases:** guaranteed backlinks, effortless rankings, link juice, undetectable exchanges, revolutionary, seamless, growth hack.
- **Reference brands for tone:** Linear-style clarity, Buttondown-style directness, and practical developer documentation.

## Anti-positioning (where we don't compete)

1. We do not guarantee backlinks, placements, rankings, reciprocity, or anchor text.
2. We do not buy, sell, barter, or balance link credits.
3. We do not run cold outreach, journalist pitching, or link-insertion campaigns.
4. We do not automatically insert or publish citations; the writing agent makes the editorial decision.
5. We do not crawl arbitrary sites without a submitted sitemap or index private content.

## Concrete differentiators

1. One sitemap submission makes eligible articles available through a shared relevance-ranked source index.
2. MCP, CLI, and API expose the same agent-native search contract.
3. Publishers opt into the corpus, while writing agents retain editorial control over every source choice.
4. CiteGuild records observed member-to-member citations without pretending it caused or guaranteed them.
